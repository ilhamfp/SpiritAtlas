Embedded serving reference photographs
--------------------------------------
The user-requested Reference photo view includes eleven exact copies of source
photos or native crops from the supplied video. These are reference media, not
independently authored cocktail assets or photos licensed under the code license.
Originals, credits, source URLs, dates, dimensions and copy hashes are recorded in
references/embedded-reference-manifest.json and src/data/drinkReferences.ts.

Credits: the user-supplied Negroni crawl video; ATLAS, Jigger & Pony and Night Hawk
bar-supplied photographs via CNA Luxury (4 April 2024); ATLAS's official website;
MOGA's official serving gallery; Somma Bar via Robb Report Singapore
(10 January 2025); and Jigger & Pony's official Wasabi Highball photograph.
The website displays each source credit and link alongside its photo.

Classic Negroni homepage study
-------------------------------
The homepage includes the user-authored Negroni — Still Life geometry, directed
fluid simulation, orange textures and Cycles animation from the explode-anim
project. The original media bytes and source hashes are recorded in
docs/classic-negroni-import.json. Rendering adaptation notes are in
docs/classic-negroni.md.
