## BBF evidence and exact short excerpts

**Official sample menu, file dated August 2024.** The preserved menu's Negroni entry names “West Winds Cutlass Gin, Bon Funk Amaro, Bon Funk Vermouth.” This gives a defensible historical gin brand and the published house-vermouth name. [Official menu link](https://www.bonfunk.com/s/BBF_SAMPLE-MENU.pdf), [resolved PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/66704982824532747e8abd2a/t/66c4ac11fff1ad5c10a86f0a/1724165139731/BBF_SAMPLE%2BMENU.pdf), [saved source metadata](../menus/bbf-sample-menu.provenance.json). The resolved PDF was reopened on this research pass; text extraction drops some letters, so the previously inspected page image remains useful.

**Official March 2026 events-linked sample, page 4.** Exact entry: “Gin, BBF Amaro, BBF Vermouth.” It is linked from page 15 of the venue's 2026 events kit. This supports the house components' published continuity without establishing the present gin brand or the blend's constituent vermouths. [Official events kit](https://www.bonfunk.com/s/Bar-Bon-Funk-Events-Kit-2026.pdf), [linked March 2026 sample](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qN_jg8xzekfjOzJiI8ToJf-6jvKnKqOz/view), [source metadata](../menus/bbf-events-linked-menu.provenance.json), [inspected page](../menus/bbf-events-linked-menu-page-4.png).

**Supplied original film, BBF 00:45–00:52.** Caption excerpts: “We've blended the amaros,” then “vermouths and the gin”; later, “A little bit of flamed orange,” and “caramelised zest on the nose.” The last two excerpts directly support an orange aromatic finish. They do not say that a separate orange syrup is mixed into the batch. [Original publication](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdLFBKQBUkU/), [local caption evidence](../caption-evidence.json), [native orange-preparation crop](../crops/bbf-orange-prep.png). The source publication date is not independently re-established here; these are exact local-video timestamps. Caption readings are not an independent audio transcription.

**Interpretation for the modifier row:** use the same orange's aromatic oils as an explanatory specimen. The physical garnish row may retain the observed orange; the modifier row explains its flavour contribution. Do not count this as another measured orange dose. American oak and freezing remain preparation techniques, never extra liquid ingredients.

## Negroni Express evidence and exact short excerpts

**Uncover Asia, published 12 September 2022** (article metadata; updated 14 September). The writers report tasting the cocktail as guests of Campari Group Asia and credit its creator as Mel John Chavez. Exact relevant excerpt: “Ancho Reyes Verde stirred into gin, vermouth and Campari”; the passage also names walnut bitters and rice syrup. This is the strongest complete historical recipe found for the exact drink name. It supports choosing gin and vermouth for the missing rows without choosing unsupported brands. [Contemporaneous tasting report](https://uncoverasia.com/negroni-week-singapore/).

**City Nomads, 6 September 2022.** The report also identifies Negroni Express by Smoke & Mirrors, with the same bartender and Ancho Reyes Verde, and describes “jasmine rice syrup.” Its pickle-and-olive garnish differs from the filmed Somma shishito. This is a closely related earlier serving, not a statement that every ingredient or garnish remained unchanged. [Dated tasting report](https://citynomads.com/drinking-for-a-good-cause-negroni-week-celebrates-10th-anniversary-this-year-with-60-bars-in-singapore/).

**Somma official archived menu, file created 21 March 2025.** The ordinary Negroni entry contains “Bombay Sapphire, Campari, Vermouth Rosso.” This is a venue-specific basis for selecting sweet red vermouth as the closest style for the unexplained Express vermouth. Bombay Sapphire is a different cocktail's brand, so the recommended Express label stays Gin. [Archived official menu](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/669a6a47bf163d18d7dd87d2/t/67dd50b88077496c164fae4d/1742557388683/Bar+Somma+Cocktails+menu+.pdf), [saved inspected page 7](../menus/somma-cocktail-menu-archived-march2025-page-07.png), [earlier source audit](../../qa/somma-menu-research.md). The public PDF did not render in this pass's web tool; the previously downloaded original and independently inspected page remain preserved.

**Current official May 2026 menu.** It does not list Negroni Express. Therefore the proposed Gin / Sweet red vermouth completion is explicitly historical / inferred, not a claim that the current list publishes Express. [Current official bar page](https://www.somma.world/bar), [saved May-menu provenance](../menus/somma-may2026-cocktail-menu.provenance.json).

The existing Ancho Verde, rice syrup, pickled shishito and orange-oil finish already have direct film support. The 2022 walnut bitters are a further historical candidate, but adding them is unnecessary to complete the missing base-spirit and vermouth rows. No new species, brand, recipe measure or additional syrup is invented.
