# Two further collections: original-bar research

Checked 13 September 2026. This research covers Bar Bon Funk, MOGA and Bar Somma; the companion [other-bars-research.md](other-bars-research.md) covers ATLAS, Jigger & Pony and Night Hawk. No application or model edits were made for this research.

The strongest choices are **Martinis** and **Highballs**. MOGA and Somma each publish a clearly named, meaningfully different version of both. With ATLAS Martini and Jigger & Pony Wasabi Highball, these form two groups of three distinct venues. No extra Bar Bon Funk entry is needed merely to spread venue coverage.

## Serving references and implementation boundaries

**Dirty Sake-Tini:** [MOGA's official gallery](https://www.moga.com.sg/gallery/) explicitly names the [serving image](https://www.moga.com.sg/wp-content/uploads/sites/57/2024/10/IMG_2520.jpg). Saved as [moga-dirty-sake-tini.jpg](moga-dirty-sake-tini.jpg), inspected at full native resolution. A shallow, rounded bowl sits on a thin stem; liquid is pale straw with some haze. A broad green-edged ribbon is rolled/folded around a diagonal metal pick across the rim. The green ribbon is consistent with cucumber, but the menu does not identify its vegetable or pickling treatment. The upload path suggests October 2024; the image does not establish an exact 2026 serving date. Use its observed form with this qualification.

**Salted Yuzu Highball:** the same official gallery explicitly names the [photo](https://www.moga.com.sg/wp-content/uploads/sites/57/2024/12/Salted-Yuzu-Highball-1.jpg), saved as [moga-salted-yuzu-highball.jpg](moga-salted-yuzu-highball.jpg) and inspected at full resolution. It shows a tall, slightly tapered glass, pale cloudy gold liquid, large rectangular clear ice at the rim, fine bubbles and condensation. No separate garnish is visible. The December 2024 upload is dated appearance evidence for a drink still named on the current menu, not proof that the serving never changed.

**Mirko’s Martini:** a [10 January 2025 Robb Report article](https://robbreport.com.sg/somma-cocktail-pasta-bar/) explicitly captions its image as Mirko’s Martini and credits Somma Bar. The [bar-supplied image](https://www.robbreport.com.sg/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/SOMMA-BAR-MIRKOS-MARTINI-2.jpg) is saved as [somma-mirkos-martini-2025.jpg](somma-mirkos-martini-2025.jpg). It shows a shallow rounded coupe, nearly clear pale-yellow liquid and a large green olive with small browned crisp-like pieces on a horizontal pick. It is an action splash photo, so resting geometry should not reproduce the splash. [Time Out's review, 4 June 2025](https://www.timeout.com/singapore/restaurants/somma), independently identifies cheese and olive as the accompaniment. The precise cheese, browned crisp treatment and whether the complete current serving is unchanged remain uncertain. In a [1 July 2025 bartender interview](https://www.timeout.com/singapore/news/bartender-spotlight-bar-sommas-mel-john-chavez-on-savoury-cocktails-and-the-perfect-martini-070125), Mel describes pre-batching/freezing the Martini; that is preparation, not a separate solid ingredient.

**Pine Highball:** the current recipe is strong; individually captioned serving geometry is not established. The 13-image [official Somma gallery](https://www.somma.world/bar-gallery) was downloaded and visually inspected via [contact sheet](somma-gallery-contact-sheet.jpg); its filenames are not drink names. A [30 April 2026 Spirited Asia article](https://www.spiritedasia.com/2026/04/drinkspotting-may-2026/) names Pine but captions its [group photograph](https://www.spiritedasia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Bar-Somma-Cocktails-02.jpg) only as the latest Somma cocktails. This group repeats the official gallery's drinks and cannot identify one glass as Pine. The PDF's flavour chart suggests a pale-gold, long drink but is not a serving illustration. A pale-gold highball with ice is therefore an **illustrative serving**, not a recovered photographic match. Use no invented garnish row. Plum/tea/pine may appear as explicitly illustrative expanded ingredient forms.

The current gallery's clear flared coupe with sea-grape-like beads on a shell is **unlabelled** and must not be assigned to Mirko's Martini. All 13 gallery originals and the press group image remain available for traceability, not as proof of an unlabelled drink's identity.

Source image URLs, native hashes and dates are in [serving-image-provenance.json](serving-image-provenance.json) and [somma-captioned-photo-provenance.json](somma-captioned-photo-provenance.json). These are local research references; downloading them does not imply a public redistribution licence.
