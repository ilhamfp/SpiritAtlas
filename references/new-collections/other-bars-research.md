## ATLAS — exact supporting sources

The [official Drinking page](https://www.atlasbar.sg/drinking) currently links [this full drinking menu](https://www.atlasbar.sg/storage/app/uploads/public/69d/332/f55/69d332f5526c7253694601.pdf). Page 15 names ATLAS Martini and its four components. This makes family classification direct, without inferring it from glass shape.

The separate [official ATLAS Martini recipe card](https://atlasbar.sg/storage/app/media/ATLASMartini_Recipe.pdf), publication date unstated, supplies more precise compatible details:

| Component / method | Official recipe-card detail |
|---|---|
| Gin | 60 ml ATLAS London Dry Gin |
| Vermouth | 15 ml Mancino Bianco Ambrato |
| Acid | 5 dashes Champagne Vinegar |
| Bitters | 2 dashes Scrappy's Orange Bitters |
| Glass | Waterford Martini |
| Finish | Stir over ice, strain, express lemon oils and garnish with a lemon twist |

The current menu corroborates the ingredient categories; the undated official recipe card is the provenance for brands, quantities, glass and method. Do not present the card's date as September 2026 or infer other quantities from these proportions. Its text specifies glassware but not dimensions; a V-shaped stemmed illustration and light straw tint are reasonable rendering choices, not measurements from the card.

Other current options screened: **Orange Blossom Martini** uses the venue's orange gin, blanc vermouth and chocolate bitters; **Howe's Old Fashioned** uses bourbon/scotch, ratafia, coteaux champenois and Italian bitter liqueur; **Gimlette** combines French gin, pineau des charentes, citrus cordial and lime. These provide alternatives, but the selected Martini has the clearest glass/garnish recipe evidence.

## Jigger & Pony — exact supporting sources

The [official homepage](https://www.jiggerandpony.com/) links [current BLOOM](https://jiggerandpony.aflip.in/bloom-menu-2026), whose live HTML still points to [this PDF](https://cdnm.heyzine.com/files/uploaded/v3/790674ab95e832b771ee97c18f9cd833bc92bd3b.pdf). It was freshly fetched: **6,682,304 bytes**, SHA-256 **`1af939c936dbd031a60b19f7e07516b1b3bfbbbc0652673f8d316170f9b1f7cb`**, matching the saved menu in `references/espresso-martini/`.

**Wasabi Highball:** page 23 lists Toki Suntory Whisky, wasabi and apricot soda. Page 22 carries its serving photograph. The homepage also identifies [the same current official photograph](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/55dff4d0e4b023d46479cd47/dbfba9ef-c63d-4d8f-9248-4bbce25c2f10/Wasabi%2BHighball.jpg) by drink name.

I inspected both images. The vessel is narrow, almost straight-sided, slightly wider at its thin rim, with a rounded thick foot. Golden liquid surrounds a tall clear ice form; fine bubbles and condensation are visible. An orange crescent sits inside the glass rather than on its rim. The menu does **not** name that garnish: identifying it as an apricot slice is a plausible inference from the recipe, not a confirmed ingredient identity. The background is stylized teal/yellow, so it is not neutral color calibration. No dimensions or serving volume are established.

Other screened choices: **Bloom Martini** (Tanqueray No. Ten, Cinzano Extra Dry, Cocchi Americano, lemon/lime; delicate carbonation), **Old Fashioned** (Buffalo Trace, demerara, Angostura; citrus-wheel garnish), **Mango Margarita**, and **Yuzu Whisky Sour**. Wasabi is the strongest highball candidate with a named current photograph. The source pages support useful illustration without a further image-refinement loop.
