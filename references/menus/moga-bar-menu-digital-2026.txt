WELCOME TO MOGĀ
Inspired by the bold spirit of 1920s 
Japan, MOGĀ is where the modern 
girl meets the golden age of cocktails. 
Behind our discreet doors lies a world 
of high energy, low lights, and timeless 
indulgence.
Sip, share, and step into Kiyoko’s journey 
— where every drink tells a story, and 
every night is an adventure.
Signature Cocktails
CONCEPT
Once upon a time in Tokyo, there lived a young woman named Kiyoko. 
She was a “Moga” — short for modan gaaru, or modern girl — a new 
generation of Japanese women who embraced freedom, fashion, and 
forward thinking.
But Kiyoko wasn’t just modern. She was restless, with a spark in her 
eye and a longing for more than what Ginza’s jazz bars and café culture 
This menu is a tribute to her journey.
Presented in the style of a whimsical children’s storybook, each page 
follows Kiyoko as she steps into a new country, soaking in the flavours, 
cultures, and sensations that await her. In every destination, she 
discovers inspiration — and from that, a cocktail is born. Each creation 
reflects the essence of the place she visits, reimagined through a 
Japanese lens — a subtle nod to her roots in every sip.

home. Rooted yet restless, she poured 
her stories, inspirations, and spirit into 
a bar of her own. She called it MOGĀ
