                        Welcome
At Jigger & Pony, every menuzine reflects where we are, and where
we’re headed next.

This year, we find ourselves in BLOOM.

To us, BLOOM is about becoming. It’s about flavour at its peak,
ideas in motion, and hospitality that continues to grow with every
guest that we welcome.

The truth is, not everything is always rosy. Things move fast, and
the world is constantly changing. But even then, we hold on to hope
while learning to appreciate the beauty of what’s already here.
You might notice it in the cocktails — flavours that open up slowly,
changing as you spend time with them. Hopefully, you’ll feel it in our
bar too: a little more ease, a little more connection. Some moments
are about growth, some about discovery, and some are simply about
being present — and all of them matter.

So settle in, raise a glass to this moment, and to everything still yet
to bloom.
With open arms and full hearts,
Martin, David, and Saverio
