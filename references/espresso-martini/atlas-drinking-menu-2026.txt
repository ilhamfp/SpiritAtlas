ATLAS was named for the Titan who holds the sky, an act of strength
                     defined by endurance.


Above him sit the Pleiades, a small gathering of stars that has guided
people for generations. Long before maps or calendars, they marked
when to plant and when to harvest, when to set out and when to
return, when to gather and when to begin again. They formed a shared
rhythm, recognised across cultures and carried through memory rather
                           than instruction.


In Greek myth, the Pleiades are the seven daughters of ATLAS. Each is
distinct in nature, yet bound beneath the same sky. Their stories are
cyclical rather than fixed, appearing, fading, and returning in new light.

                       NEGRONI RITUAL

Born in Florence in the early twentieth century, the Negroni stands as a resolute
classic. Gin, vermouth, and italian bitter liqueur in equal measure, first poured
in 1919 as a deliberate evolution of the Americano. As Orson Welles once
observed, “The bitters are excellent for your liver; the gin is bad for you. They
                              balance each other.”
